## PhD students

I will be recruiting PhD students to start in Fall 2027. Admission is through the Colorado
School of Mines Computer Science graduate program. If the work described on this site interests
you, you are welcome to write to me before or after you apply. It helps if you mention which of
the research areas interests you, since it makes your message easy to tell apart from the
generic ones.

You do not need a quantum background.

## MS and undergraduate students at Mines

I expect to have room for a small number of MS thesis and undergraduate research projects
starting in 2027. Please feel free to get in touch.

## Postdocs

No funded postdoctoral position is open at the moment. If you hold your own fellowship and the
work here fits what you want to do, write to me and we can look at it together.

## Mentoring

At Virginia Tech I co-mentored a PhD student on variational quantum algorithms, who went on to
the IBM Quantum machine learning group, and a master's student on quantum error correction, who
went on to a PhD program at the University of California, San Diego. At the University of
Washington I have mentored graduate students on quantum algorithms and measurement
optimization, and supervised postdoctoral work on noise and partial fault tolerance. I have
taught quantum computing at the graduate level, most recently at the University of Washington
using IBM and IonQ hardware.

## Quantum Information and Programming 101

An interactive course tool for a first course in quantum information and quantum programming.
Students commit to a prediction, watch what the physics does, and explain the difference. The
same circuits then become code that runs on a simulator or on a real quantum computer. It is
free, runs in the browser and needs no account. Units 0 and 1 are open, and the later units are
in preparation. The source is on
[GitHub](https://github.com/Linghua-Zhu/quantum-information-algorithm-101).
